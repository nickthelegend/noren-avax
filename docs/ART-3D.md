# Noren 3D object kit (Avalanche edition)

Single glazed objects for the landing, checkout, merchant, auditor and credit screens. The layout, fonts and copy
voice are Noren's (cream surfaces, one dark hero, persimmon accent); the NEAR edition's indigo lacquer is replaced
here so the two editions don't look alike and no blue is the main colour.

- **Material:** glossy persimmon stoneware: a thick, wet-looking kaki-orange glaze over heavy rounded stoneware, the
  glaze pooling slightly darker in the recesses, with warm black lacquer details (bands, handles, inlay lines). Not
  metal, not chrome, not candy plastic, not glass.
- **Palette:** main kaki persimmon `#d9772f` (glaze) with `#b85f1f` in the pools, accent warm lacquer black `#1f1714`,
  rim light warm cream `#f6e6c8`. These match the app tokens (`--kaki`, `--night`, `--cream`) in `web/src/styles.css`.
- **Face:** none. Noren is money and credit, so every object is the object alone.
- **Background:** transparent (no ground shadow), trimmed and padded to a square, webp at 640 px.
- **Subjects:** shop stall with a split curtain (hero), sealed coin with a keyhole (private payment), BTC coin with a
  padlock (collateral), credit card, receipt with a stamp (payment received), ledger book with a key (auditor),
  health gauge (credit health), empty tray (no payments yet), check badge (success), warning sign (error / setup).
